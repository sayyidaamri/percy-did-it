Our side of the story

Sometimes you have to bring everyone into the same room. Here is the truth about Percy, as told by the four who were there.

I was nowhere near the fridge. I was outside, under the lights, being zesty in public. Ask anyone. And if the punch bowl started to glow, well, some of us are just naturally electric.

I only work nights, so this whole thing is news to me. Did I bring the basil? Yes. Did I plan it? I prefer the word rehearsed. Percy would be flat without me and you can print that.

Darling, one does not steal a recipe. One inherits it at a dinner party while the host is looking for his glasses. I added the cinnamon. The rest is hearsay.

Everyone keeps asking me to relax. I have never not been relaxed. I carried the ice, I cooled things down, and I left before the sirens. Easy run, good snow.

Percy Did It comes from Good Trouble Drinks Co., a family cannery on the north shore of Lake Superior. Founded in 1986 in Duluth, Minnesota, the company spent forty years making quiet, sensible seltzers for other people’s labels. Then somebody swapped the recipe at the summer picnic. Nobody confessed, everybody asked for more, and the rest is on the record. More evidence to come.
