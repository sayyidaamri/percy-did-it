The greenest getaway

share this article
April 22, 20262 min reading

A clean getaway leaves nothing behind. That is why every can we fill is made mostly from recycled aluminum, and why the can in your hand could be back on a shelf in about sixty days.

What we have done so far

We moved the carton to uncoated board, dropped the plastic rings, and switched the Duluth cannery to wind power. The fruit pulp we cannot use goes to a dairy up the road, where the cows have developed opinions.

There is more to do. We will publish the numbers every spring, good or bad.
