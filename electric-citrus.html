Power surge

At 9:14 p.m. every light on the boulevard went out. At 9:15 they came back twice as bright, and Electric Citrus was standing under the marquee with a padlock that belonged to nobody. He says it is jewelry. The power company says it is evidence. Either way the block has been buzzing ever since.
